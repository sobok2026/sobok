Swiss Ephemeris browser integration

Stella uses @swisseph/browser 1.1.0 for Placidus house cusps and chart angles.
The package compiles the Swiss Ephemeris C library to WebAssembly and runs it
locally in the user's browser.

Wrapper source: https://github.com/swisseph-js/swisseph
