Stella uses the 2025 snapshot to pin official Simplified Chinese names and six-digit
codes for mainland province-level and prefecture-level administrative divisions.
The roster excludes Taiwan and includes Hong Kong and Macao according to Stella's
configured Chinese-language market. GeoNames supplies administrative identities,
representative coordinates, and IANA time-zone identifiers separately under CC BY 4.0.
