Administrative birthplace catalogs

Korea
Source: Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Administrative Standard Code Management System
Dataset: Full legal-district code list (법정동코드 전체자료)
https://www.code.go.kr/

Japan
Source: Statistics Bureau of Japan, e-Stat
Dataset: System of Social and Demographic Statistics municipality code list
https://www.e-stat.go.jp/municipalities/cities/areacode
Terms of use: https://www.e-stat.go.jp/terms-of-use

The Japanese catalog includes municipalities and Tokyo's special wards. It includes a
designated city as one value rather than its ordinary wards, and excludes the six
Northern Territories codes that appear in e-Stat but are not currently administered by
Japan.

Mainland China
Canonical dataset: National Geographical Names Database administrative-division snapshot,
dated 2025-12-31. The official service is published by the Ministry of Civil Affairs at
https://dmfw.mca.gov.cn/. Generation uses a pinned snapshot distributed by the
AreaCity-JsSpider-StatsGov project because the official service is not reliably available
to automated builds.
Mirror: https://github.com/xiangyuecn/AreaCity-JsSpider-StatsGov
Pinned commit: c6c6e35bea3066d674efe2cded189dc57a86e7d8
Mirror license: MIT

Hong Kong and Macau are deliberately represented by one product-level value each rather
than their subordinate administrative divisions: Hong Kong is one value and Macau is one
value.

City-equivalent top-level jurisdictions may also have an explicit selectable value under
their otherwise non-selectable group. Korea uses an audited official-code policy for
city-equivalent top-level groups; broad province-like regions are grouping-only. Mainland
China applies the same rule to Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, and Chongqing. These values use
the official short local name and an administrative-area representative coordinate.
Japanese prefecture groups and English country groups remain non-selectable.

Stella derives groups, stable official-code IDs, and local-script display names from these
sources. GeoNames supplies WGS84 representative coordinates, population metadata, and
search aliases as documented separately in GeoNames.txt. Generated source headers record
the exact SHA-256 hashes used for reproducibility.
