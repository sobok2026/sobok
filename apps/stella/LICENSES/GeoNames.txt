Stella uses populated-place names, aliases, coordinates, population metadata,
administrative identities, and IANA time-zone identifiers from the GeoNames cities1000,
admin1CodesASCII, admin2Codes, and KR/JP/CN country extracts. GeoNames enriches official
administrative catalogs with WGS84 coordinates and search aliases; it is not the source
of selectable Korean, Japanese, or Chinese administrative identities. Historical,
abandoned, destroyed, and city-section records are excluded from the English catalog.
Coordinates are rounded to four decimal places. Source snapshots accessed 2026-07-16.
