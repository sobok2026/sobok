Stella uses populated-place names, alternate names, coordinates, population metadata,
administrative identities, and IANA time-zone identifiers from the GeoNames cities1000,
admin1CodesASCII, admin2Codes, and country-specific alternate-name datasets. Historical,
abandoned, destroyed, and city-section records are excluded. The remaining data is
filtered by Stella's locale markets, reordered, and rounded to four coordinate decimal
places. Source snapshots accessed 2026-07-16.
