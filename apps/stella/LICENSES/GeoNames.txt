Stella uses selected city and country names, coordinates, administrative identities, and
IANA time-zone identifiers from the GeoNames cities15000, KR, and CN datasets. The data
has been curated, deduplicated, reordered, rounded to four coordinate decimal places,
and combined with localized display-name, administrative-group, and standard-time
overrides. Source snapshots accessed 2026-07-16.
