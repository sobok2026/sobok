Swiss Ephemeris browser integration and data

Stella uses @swisseph/browser 1.1.0 and Swiss Ephemeris 2.10.03 for planetary
and sensitive-point positions, chart angles, and Placidus house cusps. The
package compiles the Swiss Ephemeris C library to WebAssembly and runs it
locally in the user's browser.
