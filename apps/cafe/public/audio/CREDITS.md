Processing: converted to mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV; removed DC offset; normalized peaks to 0.6; added short edge fades to individual cues. Loop clips use a 40 ms wrap crossfade. The cloth loop concatenates alternating forward/reversed paper rustles before the crossfade. Playback level and rate are further adjusted in the game.
